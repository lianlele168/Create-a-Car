Plan a Create a Car conveyor cash farm and use passive income before spending on expensive crates, dealer flips, or merge experiments.

Keep a visible reserve

Do not dismantle income

Check code timing

Cash codes are strongest when redeemed before a planned income or crate milestone.

Safe planning rule

FAQ
Why focus on conveyor income first?

Because steady cash flow lets you recover from bad crate rolls and afford later upgrades.

When should I stop upgrading income?

Pause when the next upgrade takes too long to recover and a crate or garage purchase gives better practical value.

Can I use rare parts on the cash car?

Yes, but only if they improve the earning goal or you can move them safely later.
