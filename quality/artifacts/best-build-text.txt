GARAGE BUILD FRAMEWORK
Create a Car Best Build Guide

A strong garage is not one overloaded car. Split your progress into cash generation, rarity protection, and controlled experiments.

1. Cash engine car

Build one car whose job is simple: keep earning. This protects your next crate session and upgrade target.

2. Part quality upgrades

Upgrade one weak part slot at a time. Stop when the cash gain or rarity gain is clear.

3. Rare-parts showcase

Move your best rare pulls into a protected showcase slot so experiments do not break your valuable build.

4. Dealer and merge tester

Use duplicate parts and spare cash in a separate slot before risking core components.
