FAQ
When should I merge parts?

Merge only after you have duplicates or a protected backup setup.

Is MERGER active?

Some sources list MERGER active, while SuperCheats marks it expired. Test in-game and treat it as needs-check.

How do I avoid bad merge losses?

Use replaceable parts, keep notes, and never risk the core part of your income car.
