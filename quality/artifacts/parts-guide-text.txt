CRATES AND PARTS
Create a Car Parts Guide

Use crates and parts boxes more carefully in Create a Car by separating cash upgrades, rarity collection, and experiment parts.

Separate part purpose

Label every useful part as income, rarity, experiment, or spare. This makes upgrade decisions easier.

Open crates in sessions

Spend in planned sessions instead of one-click draining your entire cash reserve.

Protect high-rarity pulls

Move rare parts into a showcase or protected slot before testing dealer or merge ideas.

Use boxes to fix weak slots

A box reward is most valuable when it targets the weakest visible slot in your main car.

Stop after a clear win

When a crate improves the target slot, pause and rebuild around that improvement before buying more.

Safe planning rule

Create a Car can change after updates, so treat visible server numbers as your source of truth. Use this guide as a decision framework, then verify codes, costs, and rewards in-game.

FAQ
Which parts should I upgrade first?

Upgrade the part slot that improves cash generation or fixes the biggest weakness in your main car.

Are rare parts always better?

Not always for early progress. A rare flex part can be less useful than a cheaper part that boosts income.

How many crates should I open?

Use the calculator with your visible crate cost and keep a reserve before buying a batch.
