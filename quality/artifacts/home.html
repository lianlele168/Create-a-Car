What the official description confirms

Build and collect

The developer describes crate-based part collecting and cars built from Frames, Engines and Wheels.

Earn and display

Cars can earn cash offline, and a Showroom displays your collection.

Progress and compete

The description lists Prestige, ranked racing and Season Pass rewards.
