FIRST-HOUR ROUTE
Create a Car Beginner Guide

Learn the early Create a Car loop: redeem codes, roll parts, build a cash car, upgrade crates, and expand garage space without wasting your first rewards.

Redeem safe codes first

Start with CONVEYOR, GRIND, and FIXES, then test needs-check codes only after the high-confidence rewards are redeemed.

Build one earning car

Your first goal is not the rarest-looking vehicle. It is a working car that generates cash over time.

Record visible costs

Write down crate price, upgrade price, and cash-per-minute values from your server. Those numbers make the calculator useful.

Upgrade one weak slot

Swap parts gradually so you can tell which upgrade actually improved your build.

Save rare parts

Do not feed rare or high-value parts into experiments until you have a backup cash car.

Safe planning rule

Create a Car can change after updates, so treat visible server numbers as your source of truth. Use this guide as a decision framework, then verify codes, costs, and rewards in-game.

FAQ
What should I do first in Create a Car?

Redeem safe codes, build one car that earns cash, then use that income to fund better crates and garage space.

Should beginners use every code immediately?

Use high-confidence codes immediately. Test needs-check codes separately because sources currently disagree on some of them.

What is the biggest beginner mistake?

Spending all cash on random crate rolls before building a reliable income car.
