Create a Car Dealer Guide

Use Create a Car dealer logic cautiously by flipping spare parts and cash without risking your core income or rare-part builds.

Make a sell pile

Only consider parts that are duplicate, low-impact, or already replaced by better components.

Protect income parts

Never sell or risk the part that keeps your best earning car working.

Convert clutter into attempts

Dealer-style cash conversion is best when it turns unused inventory into a planned crate session.

Test conflicting codes

DEALER is currently source-conflicted, so test it in-game before counting the reward.

Track results

Keep simple notes on what you sold or bought so repeatable choices emerge over time.

Safe planning rule

FAQ

Is DEALER active?

Several sources list DEALER active, while SuperCheats marks it expired. The codes page keeps it as needs-check.

What should I sell first?

Start with duplicate or low-purpose parts, not rare parts or your cash-engine components.

When is dealer flipping worth it?

When it turns clutter into a clear upgrade or crate session without damaging your main builds.
